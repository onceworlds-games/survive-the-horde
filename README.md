# Survive the Horde

Move, and your weapons fire on their own. Collect XP, level up, pick upgrades and survive the night until dawn.
Solo or co-op with up to four friends.

Play: https://onceworlds.com/play/survive-the-horde

Plain ES modules and Canvas 2D in `game/`, no build. Rules and simulation are pure modules covered by `npm test`.
